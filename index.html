Difficulty progression

Each session has 15 scored questions. Above 85% advances one level. 70–85% repeats the level with missed words weighted more heavily. Below 70% starts a focused review at the same level. After every three scored stages, a non-graded rapid-recognition review is flagged for you.

The practice pool follows your two Level B Hebrew books and your teacher-created class notes, including the grammar and vocabulary actually covered in class.

  1. Hebrew → English recognition
  2. English → Hebrew recognition
  3. Mixed recognition
  4. Type the English meaning
  5. Type the Hebrew word
  6. Two-part sentence comprehension: choose the missing Hebrew word, then choose the correct English translation
  7. Match Hebrew words to Hebrew descriptor phrases and opposites
  8. Practice connectors, adjective agreement, definite noun/adjective forms, and past-tense להיות
  9. Heavy verb practice: definitions, infinitives, שם פעולה, and conjugation in past, present, and future
  10. Build short Hebrew sentences
  11. Apple Pencil handwriting recall
  12. Mixed cumulative challenge with two-part sentences, verbs, grammar, connectors, and matching
