The open opinion prompts, free composition, full stories and full remaining
exercise sets are not automatically graded. PDF page 146's original task
concerns converting past negatives into present-tense `אין` forms and is not
represented in the bank. A multiple-choice response cannot assess original
free writing, so the app presents targeted choices instead of claiming to
grade a student's written work.

Both chapters require at least two correct Hebrew-to-English interpretations
and 85% overall. Correct choices advance after 650 ms. Wrong choices reveal
the correction and wait for Next. The previous adaptive practice and local
progress keys are unchanged.
